# ASC 606-10-25-1
# Topic: Revenue from Contracts with Customers
# Framework: US GAAP
# Source: FASB Accounting Standards Codification
# Guidance Version: ASU 2014-09 (Topic 606) and subsequent amendments
# Purpose: Baseline authoritative paragraph for ingestion and embedding tests.

An entity shall recognize revenue to depict the transfer of promised goods or services
to customers in an amount that reflects the consideration to which the entity expects
to be entitled in exchange for those goods or services.

Recognition of revenue therefore depends on the completion of performance obligations,
the transfer of control of goods or services, and the measurement of transaction price.
The principles in this Topic establish a five-step model for identifying contracts,
performance obligations, transaction price, allocation, and timing of recognition.
